Privacy policy

What happens to the information you send us through this site.

Information you send

Our forms collect your name, phone number, email address, project type and any message you choose to include, along with the page you submitted from. This site does not use advertising or tracking cookies.

How it is used

We use these details only to respond to you, arrange an estimate and communicate about your project. We do not sell, rent or trade your information.

Services that help run the site

Form submissions may pass through an email or workflow service so they reach us. The footer map is provided by Google Maps and photographs load from Unsplash's image servers. Those providers may receive standard technical information such as your IP address, under their own policies.

Your choices

To update or delete information you have sent us, get in touch through our contact page.

Updates

This policy may change over time. The latest version always lives on this page.
