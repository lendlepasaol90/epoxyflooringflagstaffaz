Service areas in Flagstaff and Northern Arizona

We cover Flagstaff and the surrounding high country for residential projects, and travel further for commercial and industrial floors.

Distance matters on coating jobs here. Mountain weather changes quickly, and many systems have tight temperature and timing windows. Staying close to Flagstaff lets us start early, watch the forecast and adjust plans without losing a day.

The communities below are where we work most often. If you are outside them, give us a call; larger jobs and commercial work often make a longer trip worthwhile.

Flagstaff

From downtown bungalows and Southside homes to Continental, Cheshire, University Heights and the newer subdivisions on the edges of town. Garages, basements, shops and businesses of every size.

Doney Park

Large lots east of town with detached garages, pole barns and workshops. Many slabs here see big temperature swings and benefit from flexible base coats.

Timberline

Homes and outbuildings off Highway 89 north of Flagstaff. We often coat shop floors and garages that double as storage for trailers and equipment.

Kachina Village

Full-time homes and cabins in the pines south of town. Seasonal moisture and cold, unheated garages are common, so system choice and timing matter.

Mountainaire

Neighborhoods along the I-17 corridor. Garages, mudrooms and covered entries that take a beating from snow and mud.

Munds Park

Second homes and cabins, many unoccupied for parts of the year. We coordinate access and timing for owners who are away.

Bellemont

Light industrial, storage and distribution space west of Flagstaff on I-40, as well as nearby homes.

Williams

Homes and businesses along historic Route 66, including restaurants, lodging and retail spaces that need durable, easy-clean floors.

Winona

Rural properties east of Flagstaff, including barns, shops and garages on large parcels.

Every service, in every area

  • Garage Floor Coatings: Sealed, textured garage floors built for snowmelt
  • Residential Epoxy Flooring: Basements, mudrooms and lower levels that handle real life
  • Decorative Flake Flooring: Speckled chip floors that hide cinders and grit
  • Metallic Epoxy Flooring: Flowing, reflective floors for statement rooms
  • Polyaspartic Floor Coatings: Cures in the cold and holds color in high-altitude sun
  • Polyurea Floor Coatings: Flexible base coats for freeze-thaw and unheated shops
  • Patio & Concrete Coatings: Porches, walkways, steps and hot tub pads
  • Commercial Epoxy Flooring: Breweries, restaurants, shops and clinics
  • Warehouse Floor Coatings: Forklift-ready floors for cold industrial buildings
